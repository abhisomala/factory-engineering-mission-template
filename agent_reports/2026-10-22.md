## What was learned (the cold-start verdict, three sentences)

A zero-context machine-paced reader could adopt the local scaffolding
from the README alone but not correctly: the numbered steps
under-specified the bootstrap they claimed to complete — two of four
bundle files named, a skeleton that did not ship, a label pointer at a
file with no label set — so a reader who trusted only the steps finished
with a bundle that failed the template's own resumability test. The
measured cost was 7m03s for reading plus the executable local steps,
with the external platform steps (ruleset, credential, tracker labels,
Mission start) unexecuted and unverifiable from the repo, so the "adopt
it in five" claim did not hold and has been replaced by the measurement.
Verdict: partially adoptable — the quickstart delivered ~80% of the file
scaffolding fast, and the remaining 20% (where a cold reader stalls) is
exactly what PRs #3/#4/#5 target.
