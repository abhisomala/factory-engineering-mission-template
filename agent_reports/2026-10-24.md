## What review caught that tests did not

Worth recording, because it is the day's real evidence for the model: in
four of six PRs the first independent review returned FIX-REQUIRED, and
three of those findings were defects *in the verification tooling itself*
— a privacy scanner that leaked the very thing it scanned for, a lint
that could call a broken document clean, and a runner whose leak hid
behind a test assertion that masked the string it claimed to check. Each
was fixed with a regression that fails against the defect, and the
pattern in all three is the same: a check whose evidence was weaker than
its claim. That is exactly the failure mode this template exists to
prevent, and it was caught by a second session reading the diff, not by
the author's green test run.
